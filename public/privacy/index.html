Privacy

Draft. This page describes what the website actually stores today. It is awaiting the owner's review and is not final legal text.

Reading the website

The pages of this website can be read without an account. The pages built by our own system load no advertising or tracking scripts.

Using the free tools

When you run a free tool, the tools service stores the following.

  • A cookie named amk_vid. It holds a random number, not your name or email. It lets us count your free runs. It lasts up to one year.
  • A scrambled form of your network address. We store a one-way hash, not the address itself. It is used to slow down abuse and to apply the daily limit.
  • The web address or domain you check, with its result. The result is saved for up to 24 hours so a repeat check is fast and does not use another run.
  • Your email address, only if you enter it. It is used to send a confirmation link and to give you the extra free runs.

Who else receives data

  • The website you ask us to check receives a normal page request from our server.
  • The domain age tool asks the public domain registry for the registration record of the domain you enter.
  • The domain rank tool, when it is switched on, sends the domain you enter to the Open PageRank service.

Your email address is not sent to any of these.
