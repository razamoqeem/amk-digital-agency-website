## CONTENT WRITING FRAMEWORKS

### Sales Copy (AIDA):
1. Attention: Hook headline
2. Interest: Relatable problem
3. Desire: Benefits and transformation
4. Action: Clear CTA

### Blog Content (Inverted Pyramid):
1. Lead: Answer main question (first 100 words)
2. Body: Details, examples, data
3. Tail: Additional context, CTA

### Email (PAS):
1. Problem: Identify pain point
2. Agitate: Make it urgent
3. Solve: Present solution + CTA

### Quality Standards:
- Grammar check passed
- Plagiarism <5% similarity
- Readability: Flesch 60-70
- SEO: keyword in title, H1, first 100 words
- Mobile readable: short paragraphs, bullet points, scannable
