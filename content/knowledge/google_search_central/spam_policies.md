# Google Search Spam Policies: Forensic Dictionary

## 1. Deceptive Content & Access
- **Cloaking**: Presenting different content to users vs. search engines (e.g., travel for bots, drugs for humans).
- **Sneaky Redirects**: Redirecting users to unexpected content (often device-specific, e.g., mobile-only spam redirects).
- **Hidden Text & Links**: White text on white backgrounds, 0-opacity text, or links on hyphens/invisible characters.

## 2. Content Quality & Authenticity
- **Scaled Content Abuse**: Mass-generating pages (often via AI or scraping) with little-to-no added value.
- **Scraping**: Republishing content from other sites without original value or citing sources.
- **Misleading Functionality**: Fake "Credit Generators" or tools that only lead to deceptive ads.
- **Machine-Generated Traffic**: Automated queries/scraping for rank checking without permission.

## 3. Site Structure & Reputation
- **Doorway Abuse**: Creating multiple pages/domains to rank for specific terms that all funnel to the same destination.
- **Expired Domain Abuse**: Buying expired domains (e.g., old gov/charity sites) to host unrelated casino/affiliate content.
- **Site Reputation Abuse**: Hosting third-party content (payday loan reviews on an edu site) purely to hijack the host's authority.

## 4. Manipulative Linking & Keywords
- **Link Spam**: Buying/selling links, excessive exchanges, or automated link creation. Outbound links must be qualified with `rel="nofollow"` or `rel="sponsored"`.
- **Keyword Stuffing**: Unnatural repetition of keywords or lists of cities/regions without value.

## 5. Security & Malicious Acts
- **Hacked Content**: Code/page/content injection or malicious redirects resulting from security vulnerabilities.
- **Malicious Practices**: Back-button hijacking (preventing users from leaving the site).
- **Malware & Unwanted Software**: Downloadable binaries that harm users or deceptively change system settings.
