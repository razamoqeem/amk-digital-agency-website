## Who we are
- AMK Digital Agency is an AI-based digital marketing and search engine optimization agency.
- We build SEO systems that work with generative search engines, large language model crawlers and traditional search indexes.
- Vision: lead the next stage of search experience optimization and generative engine optimization (GEO).
- Purpose: help close the digital trust gap, connecting first impressions to real enquiries with data layers, schema structures and E-E-A-T strategies.
- Founder profile page: /owner/ (Ahmad Raza).

## What we do
- SEO: technical, on-page and off-page SEO, analytics and URL inspection.
- AI SEO and GEO (also called AEO): generative engine optimization and trust signals so AI search systems can find, understand and cite pages.
- Social and paid ads: ad copy, creative ideas and performance analysis across Google and Meta platforms.
- Email marketing and direct outreach: campaign design, sequences and outreach built for deliverability and conversion.
- Lead generation: prospect identification and conversion funnel planning.
- Strategy and research: competitor benchmarking, market analysis and reporting.

## Our approach
- Map pages to the E-E-A-T framework, align content with search intent, add clear trust signals for people and AI search systems.
- Technical crawl and Core Web Vitals audit, E-E-A-T signal structure, AI-search (GEO) optimization, topical authority and content clusters, regular performance reporting.
